Qiang Zhu, Associate Researcher/Master's Supervisor at the National Institute of Metrology, China, 
the Head of the Optical Rotation Metrology Laboratory
In June 2022, I graduated from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences with a Doctor of Engineering degree in Optical Engineering. Currently, I serve as an Associate Researcher/Master's Supervisor at the National Institute of Metrology, China, and is the Head of the Optical Rotation Metrology Laboratory. I am also a registered expert and member of ISO/TC130, a member of the National Printing Machinery Standardization Technical Committee, and a Master's Supervisor at Beijing Institute of Graphic Communication and Xi’an University of Technology.
My research focuses on the development of photoelectric precision instruments, photoelectric detection technologies, and optical rotation metrology. I had participated in six national key projects, including the National Key R&D Program and National Quality Infrastructure initiatives, as well as multiple metrology research projects. I have received one first prize and two third prizes at the provincial/ministerial level for scientific and technological achievements. I have led the drafting of two national standards and has published over 20 academic papers, with more than three indexed by EI. Currently, he supervises five master's students.
